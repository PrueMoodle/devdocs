Introductory sociology modules often emphasise foundational concepts that assist students in interpreting everyday life sociologically. Sociological Imagination is one of the central concepts suitable for microlearning:
1.	The Sociological Imagination (C. Wright Mills)
The concept lends itself well to brief, impactful instruction because it is conceptually rich but can be communicated through concise examples and relatable scenarios.
